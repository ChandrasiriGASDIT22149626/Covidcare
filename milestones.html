Project Proposal (PP)

Marks: 6%

Date: September 8th to 12th, 2025 (In-person)

The Proposal Presentation sets the foundation for the deep learning architecture and research focus.

Duration: 30 Minutes
Presentation: 15 mins
Q&A Session: 10 mins
Marking: 5 mins

Critical Instructions

  • Please be courteous and arrive at the venue at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time.
  • Bring the necessary connectors to connect your laptop to the projector.
  • Practice effectively and use the allotted time wisely. No extra time will be provided.
  • Bring three copies of the PP Justification Sheets.
  • Use the proposal presentation template provided in the Lecture Materials section.
  • Missing the assessment results in a zero grade. No reschedule requests will be considered.
