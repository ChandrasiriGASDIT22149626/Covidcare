Combined Deep Learning Approach for Covid-19 Detection

Using Cough Sound and Chest X-Ray Imaging

Read Abstract Research Team

Abstract

The COVID-19 pandemic underscored the urgent need for rapid, accessible, and reliable screening tools capable of functioning in resource-limited environments without reliance on laboratory infrastructure. This project presents a comprehensive, explainable deep learning framework that integrates two complementary diagnostic modalities: chest X-ray (CXR) analysis and cough sound acoustics, forming a unified multimodal screening pipeline.

For CXR classification, a segmentation-first pipeline isolates lung parenchyma using a U-Net model with a ResNet34 encoder, followed by a ResNet50 classifier and Grad-CAM for visual explanations. This effectively mitigates shortcut learning by restricting model attention to clinically relevant anatomical regions.

For cough analysis, a novel noise-robust component introduces Cough Phase Chaos Embeddings (CPCE), segmenting recordings into physiological phases and classifying them using a lightweight 1D CNN. This component maintains extraordinary accuracy even under severe noise conditions, demonstrating superior robustness for real-world deployment.

Both components prioritize clinical interpretability, providing Grad-CAM heatmaps and phase chaos heatmaps that align with verified physiological signatures. The complete pipeline is modular, reproducible, and specifically designed to address the key limitations of black-box medical AI systems.

Dual Diagnostic Modalities

Chest X-Ray Analysis

Utilizing a segmentation-first pipeline (U-Net + ResNet50) to enforce anatomical focus. Validated with Grad-CAM to ensure the AI focuses on clinically relevant lung regions, achieving 92.4% validation accuracy.

Cough Acoustic Analysis

Introducing Phase Chaos Embeddings (CPCE) to capture nonlinear dynamics. Processed with a lightweight 1D CNN (~85k params), it achieves 95.2% accuracy and is highly robust against background noise.
